# Net effective rent calculator

[Compare two commercial lease offers](https://invariant-ai.com/calculators/net-effective-rent)

For each offer, enter the rentable square feet, the lease term in months, the starting annual base rent per rentable square foot (RSF), the annual percentage increase, the initial months of free base rent, and the one-time build-out allowance per RSF you expect to use. Count the initial free months within the term.

## How to calculate average base rent

1. Divide the starting annual rent per RSF by 12 for the monthly rate. Apply the annual increase at months 13, 25, and each later lease anniversary.
2. Count zero base rent during the initial free months. Sum the monthly base rent per RSF for the full term.
3. Divide the sum by the term in years for average annual base rent per RSF. Deduct the build-out allowance from the sum before dividing for the second, allowance-adjusted figure.

## Example

Both hypothetical offers cover 10,000 rentable square feet and 60 months with a 3% annual increase.

| Offer | Starting rent per RSF/year | Opening free months | Allowance used per RSF | Average base rent per RSF/year | After allowance per RSF/year |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | $48.00 | 6 | $40.00 | $46.17 | $38.17 |
| B | $45.00 | 2 | $20.00 | $46.28 | $42.28 |

The totals are undiscounted and cover base rent. Compare operating expenses, taxes, insurance, utilities, and tenant-paid build-out separately. Use the actual payment schedule for later abatements, stepped rent or changes in rentable area.
